# Lecture 9: Procedural Curves, Repeat Zones & Audio-Reactive Nodes

Build a reusable signal display for the Lumen Field Station with Geometry Nodes: bars that grow from a fixed base, a beveled housing, curve-based rails and markers, and a sound-driven response synchronized to a ten-second audio clip. The complete lecture folder (Blender files, checkpoints and the preview) follows with the video.

## Files you need for the lecture

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`LumenCourse/audio/Lumen_signal_10s.wav`](LumenCourse/audio/Lumen_signal_10s.wav) | The course's ten-second signal: mono, 48,000 Hz, 16-bit. Smooth tone pulses at 120 Hz (low), 550 Hz (mid) and 2,200 Hz (high), with silence between them. Pulse centres in seconds: 1.00 low; 2.50 mid; 4.00 low and mid; 5.25 high; 6.30 mid; 7.00 low and high; 8.40 mid. Download it into your own `LumenCourse/audio` folder. Start playback at a low volume. |
